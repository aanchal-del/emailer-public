Design Guide Overview

Core Brand Principle

Power of Trust Must Be Present

Every mailer should include the "Power of Trust" statement to reinforce brand credibility and the message that India's most trusted credit card brand.

Key Standards

Brand Trust Statement

"INDIA'S MOST TRUSTED CREDIT CARD BRAND, 11 YEARS IN A ROW, ACCEPTED BY OVER 4.9 MILLION MERCHANTS"

As per 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 Brand Trust Report in India by TRA Research (A Consistent Group Company)

Registered Trademark Notice

All American Express cards must include the registered trademark: ® Registered trademark of American Express Company.

Copyright Notice

© 2026 American Express Company. All rights reserved.

Layout & Structure

Pre-Header

Pre-Header Formatting

CRITICALAlways end with a full stop (period).

Pre-Header Alignment

The pre-header must be aligned to the left and vertically centered to match the grey height bar.

Pre-Header Balance

The pre-header text should be centered and equal length to the right-side text "No images? View this online"

Example:
Pre-header: "Welcome to exclusive benefits."
Right side: "No images? View this online."

Header Section

Header Case Requirements

CRITICALAll headers must be in Title Case. First letter of all words should be capitalized.

Header Content

Headers should be grammatically correct and convey the main message of the emailer.

Layout Principles

Avoid Using Boxes

IMPORTANTMinimize or completely avoid box layouts in creatives. Opt for cleaner, more modern design approaches.

Use Icons Instead of Boxes

Use icons as much as possible to represent benefits and features instead of enclosed box designs.

Color Usage

Avoid using too much grey in the creative. Keep the design vibrant and focused on card-specific colors.

Responsive Design

Line Breaks

All line breaks must make sense. Do not cut card names or important information across lines unnecessarily.

Card Names

CRITICALCard names must be displayed in a single line and should never be cut off or split across lines.

Typography & Text Guidelines

Case Rules

Sentence Case

Copy in sentence case must end with a full stop (period).

Title Case

Copy in Title Case must not have a full stop at the end.

Log In Formatting

"Log In" should be written in Sentence Case format.

Card Member vs Membership

IMPORTANT"Cardmember" should be written as two separate words: "Card Member" and "Card Membership"

Special Characters & Formatting

Asterisks on Numbers

CRITICALAsterisks should appear on all numbers that have footnotes or explanations.

Asterisk Styling

Asterisks should NOT be hyperlinked and should be in the same color as the body copy.

Superscripts

Superscripts must match the relevant pointers in the body copy. If "5X³" is mentioned, the superscript "³" should correspond to a footnote explaining it.

Registered Trademark Symbol

The registered trademark symbol (®) should appear on all American Express brand references.

American Express® Cannot Be Split

CRITICAL"American Express®" must always appear as one unit and cannot be split across lines.

T&C Abbreviations

In "T&Cs apply", the letters T, C & A should be in UPPERCASE: T&Cs Apply

Character Limits

Character Limits by Element

Follow specific character limits for:

  • Pre-header text
  • Header/Title text
  • Body copy
  • CTA button text

Language & Spelling

UK English Default

Use UK English for copy unless otherwise stated for specific vocabulary (e.g., regional terms).

Spelling & Grammar

CRITICALAvoid all spelling, grammar and punctuation errors in copy.

Imagery & Visual Elements

Image Requirements

Indian Images Required

IMPORTANTAll images used in emailers should be Indian in context and relevance. Preference should be given to images from the Brand Box first.

Image Sourcing Priority

1. Brand Box (internal approved images)
2. Indian stock imagery
3. Custom Indian photography

Icons & Visual Assets

Maximize Icon Usage

Use icons as much as possible throughout the emailer to represent benefits, features, and key messages. Icons should replace or supplement box-based designs.

Registered Logo

CRITICALThe registered American Express logo should appear on all American Express card emailers.

Design Principles

Card Color Implementation

"Don't live life" tagline should use the respective card color to maintain visual consistency.

Color Consistency

Maintain the primary brand blue (#003F87 or approved variant) alongside card-specific accent colors.

Content Guidelines

Call-to-Action (CTA)

CTA Button Requirement

IMPORTANTA CTA button should be present depending on the type of emailer. The button text and styling should match the emailer's purpose.

CTA Examples by Type

Promotional: "Apply Now", "Learn More"
Servicing: "Update Profile", "View Details"
Educational: "Discover Benefits", "Explore More"

Hyperlinks

Hyperlink Text

When using phrases like "click here", the word "here" must be hyperlinked. Example: "Click here to apply."

Body Copy

Copy Quality

All body copy should be clear, concise, and benefit-focused. Explain the value proposition clearly to the reader.

Benefits Presentation

Benefits should be highlighted prominently, ideally with icons and clear visual hierarchy.

Mailer-Specific Rules

Servicing Mailer Restriction

IMPORTANTServicing mailers should NOT have "Unsubscribe" in the bottom footer. Other mailer types follow standard footer guidelines.

Footer & Closing Section

Footer Address

Address Update Requirement

CRITICALThe address in the bottom closing part of the emailer should always use the updated address with correct line breaks.

Company Attribution

Right-Aligned Company Name

American Express Banking Corp. should be aligned to the right in the footer.

Copyright & Legal

Full Legal Notice

Include all three elements in the footer:

American Express Banking Corp.

Footer Links & Unsubscribe

Standard Footer Layout

Footer links should be in Title Case and centered or left-aligned depending on design:

Privacy  |  Contact Us  |  Update Email  |  Unsubscribe

Card-Specific Design Rules

Plat Charge Cards

Plat Charge Signature

IMPORTANTPlat Charge creatives should have SK Signature at the end of the body copy, not the standard American Express Banking Corp. footer.

Port Cards

Port Card Footer

IMPORTANTAll Port Cards should have American Express Banking Corp. at the end of the body copy (standard footer).

General Card Requirements

Card Name Formatting

CRITICALCard names should:
• Be displayed in 1 line
• Never be cut off or split
• Be grammatically correct

Card Fee Disclosure

Card fees should be mentioned as per the specific card details. Include asterisks with footnotes explaining any exceptions or conditions.

Card-Specific Colors

Use the respective card's brand color for call-to-action buttons and emphasis elements like the "Don't live life" tagline.

Card Benefits Section

Benefits Display

Card benefits should be presented with icons rather than boxes. Examples include:

• Access to 1,550+ airport lounges with Priority Pass™
• 5X³ rewards on select merchants
• 100%⁴ fee waiver on annual renewal

'C' Capitalization Rule

Capital 'C' Requirement

IMPORTANTThe letter 'C' should always be in capital when referring to card features, card type, or any card-related content. Example: "Card Member" not "card member".

Quick Reference: Do's & Don'ts

✓ DO's

  • Always include "Power of Trust" statement
  • Use Title Case for headers
  • End sentence case copy with a period
  • Use Indian imagery from Brand Box first
  • Include asterisks on all numbers
  • Use icons instead of boxes
  • Keep card names on one line
  • Include registered trademark (®)
  • Use UK English for copy
  • Hyperlink "here" in "click here"
  • Match superscripts to footnotes
  • Include CTA button when appropriate
  • Use updated address in footer

✗ DON'Ts

  • Don't skip "Power of Trust" message
  • Don't use sentence case for headers
  • Don't end Title Case with period
  • Don't use non-Indian imagery
  • Don't forget asterisks on numbers
  • Don't use excessive boxes in design
  • Don't split card names across lines
  • Don't miss the ® symbol
  • Don't mix English variations
  • Don't leave "here" unhyperlinked
  • Don't use mismatched superscripts
  • Don't omit CTA when needed
  • Don't use outdated addresses
  • Don't use too much grey
  • Don't hyperlink asterisks
  • Don't include Unsubscribe in servicing mailers
